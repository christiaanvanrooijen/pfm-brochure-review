# Project context summary

## Confirmed direction

PFM wants to digitise its commercial experience, replacing fragmented presentations, old-style brochures and one-pagers with an interactive online experience.

The experience should be useful:

- in live sales meetings;
- at events and trade shows;
- as a public website experience;
- in secure customer follow-up;
- as the entry point towards solution configuration and quotation.

## Supported verticals over time

- Retail
- Retail Property: shopping centres, outlet centres and retail parks
- QSR and drive-thru
- Cities and high streets
- Museums and visitor attractions
- Automotive dealerships

Retail is the first full demo vertical.

## Core conceptual shift

Do not create a conventional website or digital PDF library. Use a spatial, guided location journey. The user moves from the market and catchment towards the physical location, entrance, inside behavior and a useful decision.

The commercial journey is:

`Context → Measure → Understand → Prove → Configure → Act`

## Data layers

- Mobile and geo data can add catchment, geographic origin, frequency, cross-visitation and surrounding-location context.
- PFM physical sensing supplies exact or clearly defined measurement at the location.
- Customer business data can add POS, transactions, revenue, staffing, events, appointments and operating context.
- Derived insight can include capture, conversion, comparison, opportunity periods and anomalies when definitions align.

These layers must never be visually collapsed into one certainty level.

## Build to believe

The experience should support commercial confidence through:

- short videos;
- contextual cases;
- approved customer logos;
- factual proof;
- technology/method explanation;
- privacy and reliability explanation;
- a concise Why PFM story.

## Platform modes

### Sales Mode

Start from an existing Odoo company/opportunity or create a new prospect. Save discovery, selections and next steps back to Odoo.

### Public Experience

No customer selection at the beginning. A visitor explores and later submits company/contact details. The application matches or creates the company/contact and creates a CRM opportunity in Odoo.

### Client Room

A secure, selected snapshot of the agreed story. It is not an unrestricted document library.

## Systems

- Odoo version: 18.
- Microsoft 365 is used internally.
- AWS is the preferred infrastructure environment.
- Hybrid content storage is preferred:
  - SharePoint for source content, formal documents and approval;
  - AWS Experience CMS/database for interactive published content;
  - Odoo for customer and commercial process.

## Digital Quote Builder

An initial Digital Quote Builder already exists. It should become part of the Configure stage. Initially integrate it as a separate module through a stable input/output contract; do not discard or duplicate its existing business logic.

## First decision milestone

Create a short, polished Retail go-demo for leadership. Its purpose is to earn approval for further development, not to prove production integrations.
