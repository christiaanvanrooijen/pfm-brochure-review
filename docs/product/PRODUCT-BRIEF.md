# Product brief

## Product statement

PFM Commercial Experience turns PFM's fragmented commercial materials and tools into one visual, guided and reusable product experience for public visitors, sales teams and customers.

## Problem

PFM's commercial story is currently distributed across presentations, brochures, one-pagers, case material, individual sales approaches and standalone tools. This creates inconsistent storytelling, duplicated content, slow preparation and weak continuity from discovery to quotation and follow-up.

## Product vision

A user starts with a location or business question and moves through a clear visual journey. PFM progressively adds the right lenses: mobile and geo context, exact physical measurement, connected business data and derived insight. Proof builds confidence, configuration makes the proposal concrete and Odoo preserves the commercial follow-up.

## Primary users

### PFM salesperson or account manager

Needs a fast, confident and visually strong conversation tool that also records useful discovery and next steps.

### Public website visitor

Needs to understand what PFM can reveal about a physical location without first knowing sensor products or analytics terminology.

### Customer stakeholder

Needs a secure, concise replay of the relevant question, solution, evidence, configuration and next step.

### Content owner

Needs controlled, reusable and approved content rather than duplicated slides and documents.

## Product modes

### Sales Mode

- Find an existing Odoo company/opportunity or create a prospect.
- Run guided or free exploration.
- Save selected challenges, lenses and capabilities.
- Open the Digital Quote Builder with prepared context.
- Update Odoo and publish a client-room snapshot.

### Public Experience

- Explore by vertical, challenge or location journey.
- No customer selection at the beginning.
- Convert meaningful intent into a qualified lead or CRM opportunity in Odoo.

### Client Room

- Show an approved and time-bound snapshot.
- Include selected visual stories, videos, cases, documents, configuration and next steps.
- Never expose internal Odoo data, draft content or the unrestricted content library.

## Initial vertical

Retail is the first vertical because it can demonstrate the full layered story:

- market and catchment;
- passing movement;
- entrance visits and capture;
- in-store routes and dwell;
- POS and staffing context;
- conversion and portfolio comparison;
- configuration and quotation.

## Commercial narrative

`Context → Measure → Understand → Prove → Configure → Act`

## Success for the go-demo

A leadership audience understands within minutes that this is not a website full of brochures, but a central commercial product that can improve consistency, lead quality, sales conversations, quotation flow and customer follow-up.
