# ARC positioning and object detection objective

Source: direct product-lead/user instruction in this conversation on 5 November 2026, accompanying the ARC, About PFM and Privacy screenshots.

## Supplied statements

- PFM’s objective is 98% accuracy in object detection.
- Reliable and consistent data has been central to PFM for years.
- This foundation becomes more important as organisations adopt AI and agentic business agents that depend on reliable, consistent inputs.

## Copy treatment in review v4

The figure is presented as **an objective**, not as a measured customer result, achieved benchmark, contractual guarantee or universal performance claim for every technology.

Published numerical evaluation needs the applicable object classes, accuracy definition, validation method, measurement scope and operating conditions. Those technical details were not supplied in this instruction. The review therefore keeps the scope statement with the objective and does not invent a methodology or measured result.

The AI text describes PFM’s positioning and the importance of input quality. It does not assert an existing PFM agent product, live agent integration or autonomous action deployed at a customer.

The ARC expansion remains Accurate, Reliable, Consistent, as verified from the official PFM page in the earlier review round. The 98% objective is sourced to the user’s instruction above, not to that earlier public webpage.

De gebruiker heeft review v4 op 5 november 2026 expliciet akkoord bevonden. De daarin opgenomen ARC-positionering en 98%-objectdetectiedoelstelling zijn daarmee als reviewcopy geaccepteerd. De bronnotitie blijft het onderscheid tussen doelstelling en gemeten prestatie vastleggen.
