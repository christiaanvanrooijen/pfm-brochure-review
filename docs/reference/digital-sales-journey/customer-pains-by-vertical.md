Source basis: `source-links.md`, `01-analysis/sales-enablement-input/digital-sales-journey-spreadsheet-extraction.md`, `01-analysis/reference-extractions/retail-reference-extraction.md`, `shopping-centre-reference-extraction.md`, `travel-reference-extraction.md`, `geolocation-cross-vertical-extraction.md`, `public-locations-reference-extraction.md`, `qsr-reference-extraction.md`, `smart-buildings-reference-extraction.md`, `03-shared/domain-knowledge/geolocation/`, `03-shared/company-profile/`, fit-signal files, `geolocation-fit-by-vertical.md`, and `cross-vertical-knowledge/`.

Confidence levels:

- `confirmed`: supported by sales enablement input and/or retail reference extraction.
- `source-backed`: directly supported by processed reference material but not necessarily PFM proof.
- `source-informed`: supported by adjacent reference material and useful for discovery, but needs PFM validation.
- `likely`: supported by adjacent evidence and PFM proposition, but needs validation before customer-facing proof language.
- `assumption`: plausible for the vertical, but current evidence is light.
- `needs validation`: keep in discovery until sales, marketing, legal, privacy or product review confirms it.

## Unclear Segment Fit

If the customer does not fit one of the six visible segments, do not force a customer-facing story. Capture the customer's own words, identify the decision they need to make, select the closest KPI family and route the opportunity for human sales review.
