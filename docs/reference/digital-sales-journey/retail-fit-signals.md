## Confidence Legend

- `confirmed`: supported by at least one retail reference and/or the sales enablement extraction.
- `likely`: supported by adjacent retail evidence and PFM value proposition, but should be validated before external proof language.
- `assumption`: plausible but not yet supported by current sources.
- `needs validation`: requires sales, marketing, legal, privacy or product review before use.
