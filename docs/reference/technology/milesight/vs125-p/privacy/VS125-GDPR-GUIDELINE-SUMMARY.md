# Milesight VS125 GDPR guideline: source summary

- **Source ID:** `SRC-MILESIGHT-VS125-PRIVACY-001`
- **Original:** https://resource.milesight.com/milesight/iot/document/whitepapers/vs125-gdpr-guideline.pdf
- **Scope:** Manufacturer guideline for the VS125; mapped to the VS125-P family with exact firmware/configuration still requiring deployment validation.
- **Evidence class:** Vendor-authored statements, not independent certification or legal review.

## Relevant evidence

- **pp. 7–8:** Binocular imaging produces depth maps; RGB images are used to detect human contours/targets. Preview options include depth video, a black-and-white still frame, and a no-image view using trajectories.
- **pp. 9–10:** The guideline describes HTTPS and digest authentication for management, encryption/TLS, local/on-device processing and storage, manual deletion, and says data are not shared with third parties absent consent or legal requirement.
- **pp. 7–8:** The manufacturer calls the VS125 GDPR-certified/compliant. This is the manufacturer's own assertion, not an independent assessment.

## Limits

This document does not show that any PFM site has selected the no-image mode, does not specify automatic deletion or a retention duration, and does not assess a particular controller's lawful basis, signage, DPIA, access policy or legal compliance. Do not describe the VS125 as image-free: the document explicitly describes imaging and selectable preview modes.
