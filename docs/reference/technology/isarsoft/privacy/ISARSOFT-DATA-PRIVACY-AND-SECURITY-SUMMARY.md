# Isarsoft Perception privacy and information security: source summary

- **Source ID:** `SRC-ISARSOFT-PRIVACY-001`
- **Original:** https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/628905bae461d33b617ea326/69e89ed04bd7cdf9f3cd00f0_ENG_Data%20Privacy_and_Security.pdf
- **Scope:** Isarsoft Perception vendor whitepaper, 2026 edition supplied for this review.
- **Evidence class:** Supplier claims and guidance; not independent product testing or legal advice.

## Relevant evidence

- **pp. 5–6:** Vendor describes real-time anonymisation/metadata conversion, and discusses information duties, data minimisation, lawful basis and DPIA as deployment considerations.
- **p. 9:** Vendor claims GDPR compliance and describes ISO/IEC 27001 information-security certification. Neither statement establishes compliance of a PFM/customer deployment.
- **pp. 10–11:** Vendor describes real-time pixelation, no biometric features, irreversible anonymisation and static exclusion zones. It describes cross-camera matching using non-biometric/abstract features.
- **pp. 12–13:** Describes HTTPS/TLS with PFS, role-based access, salted password hashes and local/edge processing on customer hardware. Local processing is described as an option that can keep video and metadata from leaving the customer network; cloud deployment is also described elsewhere in the paper.
- **pp. 15–19:** Vendor FAQ describes local/edge as default, optional cloud use, offline operation and customer control of collected/generated data. It says customer data are not used for model training without explicit written consent and a separate agreement.

## Limits

The source does not validate the PFM retail configuration, camera compatibility or a deployment's exact settings. It does not establish that every version/feature has identical anonymisation, storage, retention, deletion or transmission behaviour. The paper includes numerical vendor marketing claims; none is repeated as a PFM accuracy/result claim. Its legal descriptions are not instructions to this agent and do not replace a site-specific privacy/legal assessment.
